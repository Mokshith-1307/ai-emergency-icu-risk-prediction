### 🏥 AI-Driven ICU Risk Prediction — Mini Project

A **mini project developed as a team of three** to explore the use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning for predicting ICU patient risk levels using clinical data.

**Team Members:**

**Key Features:**

- ICU risk prediction
- Patient risk classification
- Low / Medium / High risk assessment
- Interactive Streamlit interface
- Visualization of patient risk information
